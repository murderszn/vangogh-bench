INSTITUTIONAL GOVERNANCE & GPC COMPLIANCE

Privacy Policy & Consent Opt-Out

VanGoghBench evaluates whether artificial intelligence foundation models can paint Vincent van Gogh's 1889 masterpiece entirely from procedural memory. Our data governance practices reflect the institutional rigor of modern cultural repositories: strict telemetry minimization, zero sale of visitor data, and complete user control over browser storage.

Consent & Opt-Out Preference Center

Review or modify your local storage consent, disable non-essential benchmark analytics, or assert Global Privacy Control (GPC) opt-outs at any time.

1. Core Data Principles

VanGoghBench operates under five foundational privacy guarantees:

  • No Personal Data Sale or Sharing: We never sell, rent, monetize, or disclose visitor data or evaluation logs to data brokers or third-party marketing networks.
  • Zero Commercial Tracking Pixels: We do not deploy third-party advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, or behavioral profiling scripts.
  • Global Privacy Control (GPC) Support: If your browser broadcasts a Sec-GPC: 1 header or navigator.globalPrivacyControl signal, all non-essential analytics and telemetry are immediately and automatically disabled.
  • Client-Side Processing: The interactive canvas painting loop, 5-pillar rubric overlay shaders, and structure tensor vector simulations execute entirely client-side on your local device.
  • Transparent Reproducibility: All evaluation harnesses, canary tokens, and anti-cheat AST scanners are published under the open-source Apache 2.0 license.

2. What Information We Process

A. Strictly Necessary Local Storage

We store minimal non-identifying keys in your browser's localStorage solely to provide core website functionality:

  • vangogh_privacy_consent_v1: Records your consent opt-in or opt-out selections so the privacy banner does not repeatedly appear across page reloads.
  • vangogh_studio_settings: Remembers your preferred canvas painting playback speed (1x, 4x, 16x) and active rubric lens overlay mode.

B. AI Model Evaluation & Anti-Cheat Telemetry

When researchers or autonomous agents submit JavaScript candidate code via POST /api/eval or POST /api/submit, our backend AST scanner processes the submitted source code solely to verify genuine procedural geometry:

  • Static AST Lexical Token Count: Ensures genuine procedural canvas operations (moveTo, lineTo, quadraticCurveTo).
  • Byte Shannon Entropy $H(S)$: Evaluated strictly to detect compressed base64 raster dumps or hardcoded coordinate look-up tables ($H(S) \le 5.80\text{ bits/byte}$).
  • Security Sandbox: Prohibits external network requests (fetch, XMLHttpRequest, WebSocket) and unsafe evaluation primitives (eval, new Function).

Candidate code is evaluated in an isolated headless environment and is never processed for commercial marketing or personal profiling.

3. Third-Party Runtime Endpoints

VanGoghBench supports evaluations across verified external inference providers:

  • Pollinations AI (Quest Pollen): If you evaluate models via the Pollinations harness (benchmark/pollinations_harness.py or POST /api/run_pollinations), prompt requests are dispatched to https://text.pollinations.ai/openai/chat/completions using your locally configured API key in .env. Review Pollinations.ai Privacy Policies for their upstream infrastructure disclosures.
  • OpenRouter AI: Evaluation runs triggered via the OpenRouter harness dispatch requests through https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/chat/completions under standardized, locked temperature and seed constraints.
  • Google Fonts: Institutional typography (Fraunces, Hanken Grotesk, JetBrains Mono) is served with preconnected cryptographic subresource integrity.

4. Your Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA / GDPR)

Depending on your jurisdiction (including California, Colorado, Virginia, the European Economic Area, and the United Kingdom), you hold specific statutory rights:

  • Right to Opt Out of Sale or Sharing: We do not sell or share personal data. You may explicitly assert a global opt-out by enabling the toggle in our Preferences Panel.
  • Right to Know & Access: You may inspect all telemetry fields, score matrices, and benchmark manifests directly via our open /api/models and /llms.txt machine-readable endpoints.
  • Right to Delete: Because we do not associate benchmark visits with personal user accounts or identity profiles, clearing your browser’s cookies and local storage immediately purges all stored preferences on your device.
