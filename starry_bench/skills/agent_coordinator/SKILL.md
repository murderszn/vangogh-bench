# Instructions
1. Deconstruct the master canvas into discrete semantic zones:
   - Zone A: Cosmic Swirl & Sky Field (Parametric vector curves)
   - Zone B: Celestial Halos (Concentric radial stroke generators)
   - Zone C: Alpine Horizon & Village Structures (Linear perspective block-in)
   - Zone D: Foreground Cypress (Vertical sinusoidal impasto layering)
2. Assign zones to worker subprocesses or asynchronous render queues.
3. Enforce canvas boundary constraints: Workers must clip path operations to their delegated region masks to prevent stroke overlap artifacts across seam borders.
4. Collate generated strokes into a synchronized execution stream formatted for `requestAnimationFrame`.
